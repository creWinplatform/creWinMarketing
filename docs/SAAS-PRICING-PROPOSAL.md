# Ryzevo pricing proposal

Decision note, 18 October 2026. The owner approved this commercial direction.
The versioned monthly catalog and a non-financial owner preference now appear in
Plan & usage. Paid entitlements and live checkout are NOT enabled. Existing pilot
customers/limits are unchanged; no automatic paid migration.

## Recommendation

Charge per project (one product/brand), with included seats; keep security,
consent, suppression and source attribution available in every tier. Start with
two paid plans, Launch and Growth. Offer Team only after usage and collaboration
needs justify it. Do not sell an unlimited/cheap lifetime plan or an unbounded
all-inclusive email/AI service.

| Draft allowance | Launch | Growth | Team (later) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly USD proposal | 29 | 79 | 149 |
| Optional yearly USD proposal | 290 | 790 | 1490 |
| Products/projects | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Included people | 2 | 5 | 10 |
| Connected social accounts | 3 | 5 | 10 |
| Bounded text AI drafts/month | 50 | 200 | 500 |
| Public single-URL HTML audits/month | 10 | 25 | 50 |
| Positioning | Founder workflow | Small growth team | Larger product team |

Yearly means paying the stated full amount upfront; it is approximately two
months free (16.7%), not the monthly price charged annually. Initially sell
monthly until live acceptance and retention are proven. The existing pilot is
NOT this trial: verified self-service signups now start a separate 14-day no-card
trial with 10 total AI operations, 3 single-URL audits, bounded records and no live
sends. Public signup stays gated off pending operational acceptance. See
SAAS-ENTITLEMENTS.md. Annual collection and Team remain deferred.

All tiers should share brand/goals, content and campaign drafts, calendar, work
items, clear approval/consent and real measurement provenance. Propose Google
measurement/source-linked product events as the core differentiator, not an
arbitrary paywall. Increase usable volume and collaboration as customers grow.
No fabricated traffic, ranking or ROI; no guaranteed leads/revenue.

## Catalog versus active rights

Current pilot caps remain 50 AI/month, 25 audits/month, 500 campaign drafts, 1000
content records, 500 work items and 10 membership records. It does not enforce
the above package matrix. Paid entitlement versioning must be implemented and
tested before selling different limits. An audit here means a public single-URL
HTML check, NOT 500-page crawling, PageSpeed or Search Console index coverage.

Social/SES/Google integrations have implemented pilot code but require provider
access and live acceptance before commercial claims. Supported formats are
bounded; do not advertise video, reels, full engagement inbox or unlimited
networks. AI prospect discovery and maritime/CV/Bullseye business flows are
Crewin-pack specific today and are NOT general SaaS plan inclusions. Team is
not an agency multi-project bundle; org-wide billing and white-label remain out
of this proposal.

## Variable costs and validation

- Customer-owned SES/other provider costs, advertising budgets, SMS/WhatsApp,
  paid data/enrichment, image/video generation and any provider API fees stay
  separate and explicit. No hidden automatic overage charges. Consent and domain
  verification remain required, regardless of plan.
- Keep AI work token/input/output bounded and measure actual cost per operation.
  Draft counts are product limits, not a promise of infinite model tokens. Images,
  premium models and research are not silently charged as one cheap text draft.
- Initial gross contribution target: at least 75-80% after payment, compute,
  provider/API and ordinary support cost. This is a target, not measured margin.
  A 29 USD plan at an ASSUMED 3% processor rate costs 0.87 USD in processing;
  that is a scenario, not a PayTR quote. At 80% contribution, the total variable
  cost budget would be 5.80 USD, leaving 4.93 USD after that illustrative fee.
  Include fixed/per-transaction fees if the actual contract has them.
- Validate with 5-10 non-Crewin customers: qualified activation, weekly active
  projects, time to first useful campaign, trial-to-paid, churn, AI/support costs
  and attributable measured conversions. Revisit prices after 30-60 days of
  observed usage. Use a time-limited founding discount only with explicit renewal
  pricing, not an irreversible lifetime promise.

## Market anchors, not feature-equivalent comparisons

Official prices checked 18 October 2026:

- [Buffer monthly pricing](https://support.buffer.com/articles/buffer-pricing-and-features-6pJrOPuzIt):
  first ten Essentials channels 6 USD/channel/month, Team 12 USD/channel/month.
  Three Essentials channels would be 18 USD/month; social-only functionality is
  not identical to Ryzevo's proposed workflow.
- [Brevo plans](https://help.brevo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208589409-About-Brevo-s-pricing-plans):
  Starter from 9 USD/month and Standard from 18 USD/month. Volume, add-ons and
  included services differ. Do not claim Ryzevo replaces all Brevo features.
- [AWS SES pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/ses/pricing/): provider costs are
  usage-based and may include additional data/deliverability services. Avoid an
  unlimited hosted-email promise based only on its basic outbound rate.

The proposed 29/79 price points are an experiment for an integrated startup
growth workflow, not established willingness-to-pay or a guarantee of lower
total cost. Price advantage alone is not a substitute for live reliability,
useful activation and a clear cross-channel conversion workflow.
